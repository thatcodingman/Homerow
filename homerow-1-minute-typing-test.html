About the 1 minute typing test

A one-minute test is one of the most popular ways to measure typing speed. It is long enough to smooth out a fast start or a slow warm-up, and short enough to repeat several times in a row. Home Row scores your words per minute (WPM), accuracy, raw WPM, errors, and consistency, then shows a keyboard heatmap of the keys you missed most.

What is a good score on a 1 minute typing test?

  • Under 30 WPM — beginner, still building muscle memory for key positions
  • 35–45 WPM — average for most adults doing everyday typing
  • 50–65 WPM — solid, comfortable touch-typing speed
  • 65–90 WPM — fast, well above the office-work average
  • 100+ WPM — professional transcriptionist or competitive-typist territory

Tips for your best minute

  • Take a moment to settle your hands on the home row before you start
  • Don't sprint the first ten seconds, because a pace you can hold for the whole minute beats a burst you can't
  • Keep your eyes on the text, not the keyboard
  • If you make a mistake, fix it and keep going; correcting a typo doesn't hurt your score, but it does cost time
  • Run the test a few times and judge yourself by your average, not one result

Try a different length

Want something shorter or longer? Try the 30 second typing test, 2 minute typing test, 3 minute typing test, 5 minute typing test. For tips on raising your score, read how to type faster.

1 Minute typing test FAQ

How is the 1 minute typing test scored?

Because the test lasts a full minute, your WPM is your correctly typed characters divided by 5. Raw WPM counts every character you typed, mistakes included. The FAQ explains the formulas in full.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account or login, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

Can I change the text style?

Yes. Use the Words, Quotes, and Punctuation buttons above the test to switch between plain words, sentences, and text with capitals and punctuation.
