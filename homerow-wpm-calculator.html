Words per minute, accuracy, and characters per minute

Typed something outside Home Row and want to know your speed? Enter what you typed and how long it took. Nothing is sent anywhere; the math runs in your browser. For a live measurement, take the typing test.

Enter characters and a time to see your result.

How the calculation works

  • Raw WPM = characters typed ÷ 5 ÷ minutes
  • Net WPM = (characters typed − mistakes) ÷ 5 ÷ minutes
  • Accuracy = (characters typed − mistakes) ÷ characters typed
  • Characters per minute (CPM) = characters typed ÷ minutes

One "word" is standardized as 5 characters, spaces included, so WPM does not depend on how long your words happen to be. This matches how Home Row scores its own test; the FAQ has the details.

Counting words instead of characters

If you only know the word count, multiply it by 5 to estimate characters. A 300-word document typed in 6 minutes is about 1,500 characters, or roughly 50 WPM before subtracting mistakes.

What is a good result?

Around 40 WPM is average and 60 or more is good. See what is a good typing speed for the full benchmarks, or how to type faster to improve.
