Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Home Row is built to need as little of your data as possible.

1. What happens to what you type

The passage you're tested on and every character you type are processed entirely in your browser's memory. None of it is ever transmitted, uploaded, or stored on a server. Refreshing or closing the tab discards everything instantly.

2. Cookies and analytics

Home Row uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns — which pages get visited and roughly how many people use the site. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. It has no access to your typing, your results, or your personal best, since none of that is ever sent to us in the first place. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking site-wide using Google's browser add-on, or block it with most ad-blocking extensions. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google Analytics handles data.

3. Accounts and data storage

There is no account system. Home Row saves a few small things directly in your browser using a standard feature called local storage: your personal best WPM for each test length (so it can show you "new personal best" when you beat it), your last 30 results (WPM, accuracy, mode, and test length) used for the "Recent" row and your progress panel, a daily practice streak count with the date you last tested, and your text-size choice. None of it leaves your device — it's not sent to us, not tied to your identity, and clearing your browser data, or using the "Clear my data" button under the test, removes it completely.

4. Advertising

Home Row may show ads served by Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. When ads are active, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your browsing history.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or by visiting aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less tailored to you. Ads are never shown in the middle of a typing test, and the ad vendors have no access to what you type, since that never leaves your browser. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services for more detail.

5. Children's privacy

Home Row is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from anyone, of any age, since no personal information is collected at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes — like adding new analytics, advertising, or data collection — will be described plainly here.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pausedawg@gmail.com.
